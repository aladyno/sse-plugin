Key: PROJ-9001
Type: Story | Priority: Critical | Labels: payment, orders

Summary: Let guest customers cancel an order within 24h with an automatic refund

Description:
Today a guest customer (checkout without an account) who wants to cancel must email
support. Requirement: add a "Cancel order" link to the order confirmation email. When
the customer opens the link within 24h of placing the order and before anything has
shipped, the system cancels the order and refunds the card used for payment according
to the store cancellation policy.

Acceptance Criteria:
1. The cancel link works for 24h from order creation (store local time).
2. Orders that have shipped, or are outside the policy window, show a "cannot cancel" message.
3. The refund is the amount paid minus the cancellation fee; the refund amount is shown before the customer confirms.

Latest comment (Product Owner): "24h is measured from order creation in UTC, not store local time."
