# Demand playbook

Read on demand. Generic ways to walk the positioning-to-payback chain with real data.
Project facts (real CAC, real customers, real win/loss) override every default here.

## 1. Walking the chain — what to collect per link

| Link | Direct evidence | Acceptable proxy (label it) | Measure to replace the proxy |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICP | Top 20% customers by retention/expansion, with shared traits | Sales team's description of "easy deals" | Pull retention and expansion by segment from billing/CRM |
| Job & alternative | Customer interviews (switch interviews), win/loss notes | Support tickets and review-site quotes | 5–10 switch interviews with recent wins and losses |
| Position | One sentence agreed by CEO, product, sales, marketing | Homepage headline | Ask 5 people across functions to write it; compare |
| Channel | First-touch and opportunity source from CRM timestamps | Self-reported "how did you hear about us" | Fix attribution fields; review after one full sales cycle |
| Payback | CAC and gross-margin payback per channel | Blended CAC | Split spend and new customers per channel for 2–3 quarters |

Rule: stop at the first link with no direct evidence — fixing later links first optimises a guess.

## 2. Per-channel economics table

| Channel | Spend / period | New customers | CAC | Gross-margin payback (months) | Trend vs. last period | Posture |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| … | … | … | spend ÷ customers | CAC ÷ (monthly revenue × gross margin) | ↑ / ↓ | scale / fix / cut |

Postures:
- **Scale** — payback within the company's target and stable or improving; capacity left in channel.
- **Fix** — payback drifting up; find whether volume, conversion or deal size changed.
- **Cut** — payback beyond ~18 months with no improving trend, or channel not in the strategy.

Common errors: revenue instead of gross margin in payback; mixing periods; ignoring the sales cost
attached to a marketing-sourced lead.

## 3. Pipeline-miss diagnosis (with sales)

Before adding budget, split the miss:
- **Volume** — too few qualified leads at the top → channel or budget issue.
- **Quality** — enough leads, low lead → opportunity rate → ICP or message issue (go back up the chain).
- **Velocity** — opportunities stall or slip → sales process issue (`sse-cro`).

## 4. Lead-definition SLA (marketing ↔ sales)

One written page: definition of a qualified lead (fit + intent), who follows up and within how many
hours, what counts as rejection and why, monthly review of rejected leads. A recurring
"lead quality" fight without this page is a system gap, not a people problem.

## 5. Brand vs. performance — two clocks

| | Brand | Performance |
|---|---|---|
| Horizon | Quarters to years | Days to weeks |
| Judge on | Share of search, direct traffic, win-rate vs. main rival, price realisation | CAC, payback, conversion |
| Failure mode | Cut first in a squeeze, then CAC rises a year later | Optimised to the last click, saturates |

Agree the split and the metrics before the budget meeting, not during it.

## 6. Growth-motion fit check

| Signal | Product-led fits | Sales-led fits | Community/content-led fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACV | Low | High | Low–mid |
| Time to value | Minutes–days | Weeks+ | Varies |
| Buyer | The user | A committee | Practitioners who talk to each other |

A hybrid is fine only when one motion is primary and owns the number.
